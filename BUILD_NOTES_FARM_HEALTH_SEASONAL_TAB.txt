PAL-AI FARM HEALTH & SEASONAL CONDITION UPDATE
================================================

Baseline:
PALAI_ACCURATE_TERRAIN_FETCH_PROGRESS.zip

Scope:
- Added Farm Health & Seasonal Condition as a separate Farm Site Analysis / Terrain subtab.
- The report automatically runs after a successful 3D terrain generation.
- Added the user-supplied assistant narration audio for this subtab.
- Added POST /api/farm-condition.

Farm-level differentiation:
- Uses the selected terrain latitude and longitude.
- Requests coordinate-specific daily and monthly NASA POWER climate data when internet is available.
- Combines local temperature, rainfall, humidity, wind, solar energy, crop stage,
  irrigation context, terrain suitability, drainage, and irrigation potential.
- Falls back clearly to the regional PAL-AI climate proxy if NASA POWER is unavailable.
- Does not claim direct satellite NDVI, EVI, or LST measurement.

Farmer-facing result:
- Overall Farm Condition score and status.
- Main concern and explanation.
- Vegetation, moisture, heat, and seasonal-fit indicators.
- Current 30 days vs previous 30 days vs usual conditions.
- 12-month condition outlook.
- Prioritized farmer action plan.
- Collapsible scientific details and source disclosure.

Preserved:
- Existing calibrated yield formulas.
- Real DEM terrain rules and SQLite cache.
- Long-term planting calculations and PDF.
- Soil and fertilizer analysis.
- Pest and PALADIN functions.
- No synthetic terrain fallback and no offline DEM raster.
