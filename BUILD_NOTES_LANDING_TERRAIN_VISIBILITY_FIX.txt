PAL-AI LANDING TERRAIN VISIBILITY FIX

Baseline:
PALAI_VISUAL_CONTEXT_AND_LANDING_TERRAIN_UPDATE.zip

Problem fixed:
The mock landing-page terrain canvas was drawing the projected screen X coordinate and then accidentally overwriting it with the terrain grid's model-space X value. This collapsed the mesh against the left edge of the canvas, leaving the independent scanner animation visible while the terrain appeared missing.

Changes:
- Preserved projected canvas coordinates by storing model coordinates as modelX/modelZ.
- Kept the complete yellow-green terrain surface visible throughout the loop.
- The scanner now brightens the mapped portion instead of controlling basic terrain visibility.
- Moved the atmospheric footprint behind the mesh.
- Strengthened surface opacity, grid contrast, and responsive visibility.
- Preserved slow rotation, natural mountains/slopes, suitability gradient, and all project functions.

No backend, forecast, terrain-analysis, pest, soil, chatbot, or PDF calculation was changed.
