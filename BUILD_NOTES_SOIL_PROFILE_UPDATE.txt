PAL-AI SOIL PROFILE AND CLASSIFICATION UPDATE
Baseline: PALAI_LONGTERM_UI_PDF_FINAL_FIX.zip

Scope
- Expanded only the 3D Terrain soil interpretation and its fertilizer context.
- Preserved long-term planting predictions, planting PDFs, backend forecasting, calibrated yield formulas, terrain elevation rules, SQLite cache, and preload scripts.

New soil inputs
- Optional known soil texture
- Optional measured soil pH
- Optional organic matter percentage
- Optional observed drainage class
- Evidence source: terrain only, field observation, official/published map, or laboratory test
- Optional soil test or observation date

New terrain results
- Likely/known soil texture
- Probable terrain-derived soil group
- Water-retention class
- Effective drainage class
- Rice-soil suitability score and label
- Evidence source and confidence level
- Evidence summary, limitations, and management recommendations
- Explicit disclaimer distinguishing terrain inference from laboratory confirmation

New 3D mode
- Soil & Drainage Zones
- Blue: water-retention/waterlogging tendency
- Green: balanced drainage
- Yellow: faster drainage/leaching tendency
- Orange/red: erosion and shallow-soil risk
- This is a terrain-derived proxy, not a laboratory or official soil classification map.

Fertilizer integration
- User-entered texture and pH are used when supplied.
- Otherwise the system retains terrain-derived estimates.
- Fertilizer compatibility now reports the soil evidence source.
- Exact fertilizer and lime rates still require laboratory soil testing.

Important invariants
- Soil evidence does not change DEM data.
- Soil evidence does not change topography score, yield-impact score, terrain modifier, or calibrated yield formulas.
- No synthetic terrain fallback was added.
- No offline DEM raster was added.
