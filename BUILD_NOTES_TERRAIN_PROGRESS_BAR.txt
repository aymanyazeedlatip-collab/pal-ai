PAL-AI Terrain Fetch Progress Bar Update

Changed only the 3D Terrain loading-status interface.

The terrain status panel now reports actual completed elevation-grid points:
- Completed progress = processed points / total requested points
- Current batch is shown as a separate animated pending segment
- Real DEM points received are shown separately from processed request points
- Batch number and total batches are displayed
- Validation and 3D-building stages are displayed after fetching reaches 100%

No elevation source, cache, interpolation threshold, terrain formula, yield formula,
or backend behavior was changed.
