PAL-AI Water Overlay Orientation Fix

Confirmed cause:
- The elevation grid and water geometry both correctly used increasing latitude as +Z (north).
- The 2D Water Body Analyzer is north-up.
- The default 3D camera was positioned on the north side (+Z) looking south, so the complete terrain and water overlay appeared vertically reversed compared with the north-up analyzer.

Fix:
- Moved the initial, post-build, and Reset Camera positions to the south side (-Z).
- Kept the real latitude/longitude-to-grid projection unchanged because it was already correct.
- The default 3D view is now north-up and matches the Water Body Analyzer orientation.
- The arrow compass continues to project true north on screen and remains correct after orbiting.

No elevation, water-body, terrain-scoring, soil, forecast, or yield calculations were changed.
